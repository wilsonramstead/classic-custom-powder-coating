Inside the shop

Every part goes in bare. It comes out built to last.

Powder coating isn't a spray-and-go paint job. Each piece is stripped and prepped down to clean metal, coated in the color you chose, then baked so the finish flows out into a smooth, hard shell that's bonded to the part — not just sitting on top of it.

Classic Custom is an owner-run shop in Tampa, and it shows in the work. Customers — including other business owners — call Jay "the best there is around," pointing to clean, durable results, fair pricing and a turnaround that's hard to beat.
